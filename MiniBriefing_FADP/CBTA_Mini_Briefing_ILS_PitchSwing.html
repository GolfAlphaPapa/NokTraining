Expected answers and probes

Q1. Temporary fluctuation of the glide slope and/or localizer signal, caused by other aircraft taxiing across the runway or moving through the ILS critical or sensitive areas while our aircraft is in ILS capture under autopilot control. The autopilot tracks the erroneous beam and drives rapid control surface and thrust inputs.

Q2. F/D commands look aggressive or inconsistent with the visual approach picture when cross-checked against ILS raw data.

Q3. Disconnect the autopilot and auto-throttle without delay. Do not push the control column against the engaged autopilot.

Q4. Go around immediately and without hesitation: the autopilot disconnected too low, or the aircraft no longer meets stabilised approach criteria (pitch attitude beyond limits, airspeed deviation, sink rate above 1,000 fpm).

Instructor notes

Background (bulletin section 1): from a recent FDAP review, multiple "Pitch Rate High above AFE" events were recorded during the final approach phase on B737NG fleet operations. Root cause, aircraft behaviour and the three risks are as printed in the bulletin.

The bulletin's cover figure shows a wave around the ILS glide slope line with the aircraft following it. The deck cause chain above is a restatement of the bulletin text, not a Boeing system description. For the autopilot mode logic see FCOM; this briefing does not cover it VERIFY.

The FDAP dashboard event "Pitch Rate High at Landing" (156 high, 1 Jan to 1 Apr 2026) is a different event from "Pitch Rate High above AFE" and the counts are not mixed here.

Stage 3 · Pilot action

Four techniques

1

Monitor closely below AFE

Cross-check F/D commands against ILS raw data. Aggressive or inconsistent with the visual picture: suspect the signal.

2

Disconnect immediately

Pitch swing, or thrust and pitch vary abnormally with no obvious cause: autopilot and auto-throttle off, without delay.

3

Fly manually

Visual cues first, ILS raw data once the signal stabilises, or the normal visual glide path.

4

Go around if unstable

Autopilot disconnected too low, or stabilised criteria lost (pitch, airspeed, sink above 1,000 fpm): go around, without hesitation.

CAUTION Do NOT override autopilot inputs through the control column. Disconnect the autopilot first, to avoid commanding the aircraft against an actively engaged system.

Instructor notes

All four techniques and the caution are the SSQ 013/2026 section 2 text, shortened. The bulletin says flight crew "shall apply" these techniques to prevent loss of pitch control or deviation from stabilised approach criteria. Treat as company direction; the exact autopilot disconnect method is in the FCOM and SOP VERIFY SECTION.

Stabilised approach gates and call-outs: SOP 20.3 (stabilised by 1,000 ft IMC or 500 ft VMC, sink rate no greater than 1,000 fpm). The bulletin example of unstable criteria uses the same 1,000 fpm sink rate.
